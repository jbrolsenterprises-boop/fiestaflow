Custody rule

Inventory balances are the result of recorded movements—not editable numbers. Each event should retain location, quantity, people involved, time, evidence and approval where required. Crates remain controlled custody items and cannot be lent to accounts that did not purchase them. Damaged or missing cylinders are replaced at the regular cylinder price; there is no separate damage fee.
